# Portfolio

A one-page personal site whose job is to get Huy Quoc Vo hired as a software engineer. Its reader is a recruiter or hiring manager who skims for about a minute.

## Language

**Visitor**:
A recruiter or hiring manager reading the site to decide whether to contact Huy.
_Avoid_: User, client, viewer

**Positioning**:
The plain job title (Software Engineer) plus a tagline that states the business value: faster pipelines, lower cloud cost, safer releases.
_Avoid_: Tagline, bio, intro

**Experience entry**:
One employer, with title, period, stack and a short list of result-led bullets taken from the resume.
_Avoid_: Job, work history item

**Resume**:
The one-page PDF served from the site. The public version omits the phone number.
_Avoid_: CV

**Skill group**:
A named cluster of skills (for example "Cloud & Platform") shown as a labelled list.
_Avoid_: Tech stack, skill cloud

**Project**:
A piece of work shown with a problem, what Huy did and the outcome.
_Avoid_: Demo, app, website

**Featured project**:
A Project shown first because it demonstrates backend or platform work.
_Avoid_: Top project, highlight

**More projects**:
The older, mostly front-end Projects kept behind a toggle below the Featured projects.
_Avoid_: Archive, other work

**Contact path**:
The plain, copyable email address plus an "Email me" action; the only way a Visitor is asked to act.
_Avoid_: Contact form, CTA
